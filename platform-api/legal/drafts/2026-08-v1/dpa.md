# DRAFT — AGRO-AI Data Processing Addendum

**Version:** 2026-08-v1  
**Status:** LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — NOT EFFECTIVE

This draft is a review scaffold for counsel and is not an effective DPA.

## 1. Scope and roles

This addendum is intended to apply when AGRO-AI Inc. processes personal data on behalf of a Customer in connection with Developer Services and applicable data-protection law requires processor/service-provider terms.

The final DPA must identify, for each relevant processing activity, whether Customer acts as controller/business and AGRO-AI acts as processor/service provider, or whether another role allocation applies.

## 2. Documented instructions

AGRO-AI will process covered personal data only on Customer’s documented instructions, including instructions expressed through authorized use of the Developer Services, except where applicable law requires otherwise. If legally permitted, AGRO-AI will notify Customer of a conflicting legal requirement.

## 3. Confidentiality

AGRO-AI will ensure persons authorized to process covered personal data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## 4. Security measures

AGRO-AI will maintain technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk of the covered processing. The final DPA should reference an approved security exhibit that accurately reflects production controls and must not claim certifications that have not been issued.

Security measures should address, where appropriate, access control, credential management, encryption in transit and at rest, tenant isolation, logging, vulnerability management, backup/recovery, incident response, change management, and personnel controls.

## 5. Subprocessors

Customer authorizes AGRO-AI to use subprocessors subject to the final DPA’s approved notice and objection process. AGRO-AI will impose data-protection obligations on subprocessors appropriate to the services they provide.

**COUNSEL / OPERATIONS REVIEW REQUIRED:** attach or link to the actual production subprocessor list before approval. Do not publish placeholder vendors as an authoritative list.

## 6. Assistance with individual rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, AGRO-AI will provide reasonable assistance for Customer to respond to valid requests from individuals exercising applicable data-protection rights, to the extent required by law and the final commercial agreement.

If AGRO-AI receives a request relating to personal data processed on Customer’s behalf, AGRO-AI may direct the requester to Customer unless law requires AGRO-AI to respond directly.

## 7. Security incidents

AGRO-AI will notify Customer of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting covered personal data without undue delay as required by applicable law and the final DPA. The notice should include information reasonably available to AGRO-AI that Customer needs to meet applicable notification obligations.

The final DPA must define the approved notification channel, scope, timing standard, and exclusions for unsuccessful security events.

## 8. Data protection impact assessments and regulators

AGRO-AI will provide reasonable information and assistance required by applicable law for Customer’s data-protection impact assessments or consultations with supervisory authorities, taking into account the nature of the processing and information available to AGRO-AI.

## 9. Return and deletion

At the end of the applicable services, AGRO-AI will delete or return covered personal data as required by the final agreement and applicable law, subject to lawful retention, backup, security, and legal-hold requirements.

**OPERATIONS REVIEW REQUIRED:** final wording must match actual deletion and backup-retention behavior.

## 10. Audits and information

AGRO-AI will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with processor obligations as required by applicable law. The final DPA should define a practical audit mechanism, confidentiality requirements, frequency, scope, cost allocation, and treatment of third-party audit reports.

## 11. International transfers

Where covered personal data is transferred across borders and applicable law requires a transfer mechanism, the parties will use the mechanism approved in the final DPA, which may include applicable standard contractual clauses or another lawful mechanism.

**COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED:** do not activate international-transfer representations without the correct modules, annexes, transfer-impact process, and subprocessor locations.

## 12. U.S. state privacy service-provider / processor terms

Where applicable, the final DPA should include restrictions required for a service provider, contractor, or processor under applicable U.S. state privacy laws, including limits on selling/sharing data, combining data, retaining/using/disclosing outside permitted business purposes, and handling rights requests.

**COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED:** exact statutory language and applicability must be confirmed before approval.

## 13. Processing details — to complete before approval

The final DPA must include an annex covering:

- subject matter and duration;
- nature and purpose of processing;
- categories of personal data;
- categories of data subjects;
- Customer instructions;
- retention/deletion expectations;
- approved subprocessors;
- technical and organizational measures;
- transfer locations and mechanisms where applicable.

## 14. Order of precedence

The final DPA should state its relationship to the Platform API Terms and any signed enterprise agreement. Counsel must approve the order-of-precedence language.
