# DRAFT — AGRO-AI Platform API Privacy Notice

**Version:** 2026-08-v1  
**Status:** LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — NOT EFFECTIVE

This draft describes the intended privacy disclosures for the AGRO-AI Platform API developer experience. Counsel must reconcile it with AGRO-AI’s actual production data flows, vendors, retention settings, website analytics, applicable jurisdictions, and company-wide privacy policy before approval.

## 1. Scope

This notice is intended to describe personal information processed in connection with AGRO-AI Platform API account registration, developer-console use, CLI authorization, support, security, and related developer operations. It does not by itself define processing performed under a separate enterprise contract or data-processing agreement.

## 2. Information the developer platform may collect

Depending on how a person or organization uses the service, AGRO-AI may process:

- account and identity information, such as name, email address, professional role, organization, account-verification state, and authentication metadata;
- organization and professional-verification information submitted during registration;
- developer configuration, such as projects, service-account metadata, API-key fingerprints and prefixes, scopes, webhook configuration, and integration settings;
- technical and security information, such as IP address or privacy-preserving IP-derived values, user agent, request identifiers, timestamps, authentication events, rate-limit events, audit events, and security signals;
- usage information, such as API operations, response status, latency, usage units, job state, and bounded request-log metadata;
- support information that a user submits to AGRO-AI;
- Customer Data that an authorized organization submits to or authorizes AGRO-AI to retrieve through the Platform API.

The final notice must distinguish clearly between personal information, Customer Data, synthetic TEST data, telemetry, and de-identified/aggregated information.

## 3. Sources

Information may come directly from users and organizations, from use of the Developer Services, from organization-authorized third-party integrations, from security and infrastructure providers, and from other sources permitted by applicable law.

## 4. Purposes

AGRO-AI may process information to:

- register and authenticate users;
- verify organizations and professional access;
- create and operate developer projects, service accounts, and credentials;
- provide API, CLI, SDK, sandbox, support, and related services;
- secure accounts, detect abuse, investigate incidents, and enforce access controls;
- meter and understand service usage;
- maintain logs, provenance, auditability, and reliability;
- communicate operational, security, legal, and product notices;
- comply with law and protect legal rights;
- improve Developer Services using information that AGRO-AI is legally permitted to use for that purpose.

The final notice must identify any processing purpose that is optional, based on consent, or subject to opt-out rights.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

AGRO-AI may disclose information to infrastructure, hosting, security, communications, analytics, payment, support, professional-service, and integration providers when necessary for authorized purposes and subject to appropriate contractual or legal controls.

AGRO-AI may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, in connection with a corporate transaction, or at an organization’s direction.

**COUNSEL / OPERATIONS REVIEW REQUIRED:** the final notice must state accurately whether AGRO-AI “sells” or “shares” personal information as those terms are defined by applicable privacy laws, including treatment of advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising technologies. This draft makes no approved representation on that point.

## 6. Retention

AGRO-AI should retain personal information and developer telemetry only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, including service delivery, security, auditability, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and legitimate operational needs.

**COUNSEL / OPERATIONS REVIEW REQUIRED:** the final notice must document or link to the actual retention rules for account records, security logs, request logs, support records, acceptance records, and deleted organizations/projects. Do not publish a retention period that production systems do not enforce.

## 7. Security

AGRO-AI uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Users are responsible for safeguarding credentials and for promptly reporting suspected compromise.

The final notice should remain consistent with AGRO-AI’s actual security program and must not claim certifications or audit results that have not been issued.

## 8. Privacy rights

Depending on residence, applicable law, and AGRO-AI’s legal status, individuals may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about certain personal information, and may have rights concerning sale/sharing, targeted advertising, profiling, portability, restriction, objection, or consent withdrawal.

**COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED:** the final notice must identify the rights and request methods that actually apply, including California-specific disclosures if applicable. It must also identify an operational method for identity verification and response handling.

## 9. International data transfers

Developer Services may involve processing in countries other than the user’s own. If applicable law requires transfer safeguards, AGRO-AI will use an appropriate legal mechanism.

**COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED:** the final notice and DPA must identify the actual transfer mechanism and relevant subprocessors before making a definitive statement.

## 10. Children

The Developer Services are intended for business and professional users, not children. The final notice should specify the minimum age and any jurisdiction-specific rules counsel determines are appropriate.

## 11. Organization-controlled data

When AGRO-AI processes personal information on behalf of an enterprise customer, that customer may be responsible for responding to individual privacy requests. Individuals should contact the relevant organization where appropriate. AGRO-AI will support customers as required by the applicable agreement and law.

## 12. Changes

AGRO-AI may update the privacy notice as practices or legal requirements change. The effective version, date, and material-change process must be recorded in the production legal catalog.

## 13. Contact

Until counsel approves a dedicated privacy contact and legal address, privacy questions may be directed to `support@agroai-pilot.com` for routing. The final notice must provide any additional legally required contact methods.
