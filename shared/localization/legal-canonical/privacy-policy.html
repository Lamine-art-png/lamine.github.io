AGRO-AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

AGRO-AI builds enterprise agriculture intelligence software for growers, farm operators, advisors, water districts, dealers, OEMs, data partners, and agricultural enterprises. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use AGRO-AI websites, forms, enterprise portal, APIs, integrations, reports, and related services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Contact and account information

This may include your name, email address, company or organization, job title, phone number, location, account credentials, and communications with AGRO-AI.

Organization and farm context

This may include acreage, region, crop context, farm or district information, irrigation systems used, operating requirements, and information you submit through onboarding forms, demo requests, support requests, or enterprise workflows.

Agricultural and operational data

When authorized by a customer or user, AGRO-AI may process agricultural operations data, including field boundaries, controller data, irrigation records, equipment or machine data, work records, work results, weather data, satellite or remote-sensing data, crop context, reporting records, compliance evidence, and other operational information needed to provide the service.

Integration data

When you connect third-party systems, AGRO-AI may receive data from those systems based on the permissions you approve. This may include data from irrigation systems, farm management systems, equipment platforms, weather providers, satellite or geospatial providers, compliance systems, spreadsheets, APIs, and other connected tools.

Usage and technical information

We may collect information about how you interact with our website, portal, APIs, and services, including IP address, device type, browser type, pages viewed, actions taken, timestamps, logs, diagnostic information, and system performance data.

Payment and billing information

If paid services are used, billing information may be processed by our payment or billing providers. AGRO-AI does not need to store full payment card numbers unless expressly required for a specific enterprise arrangement.

2. How we use information

AGRO-AI uses information to:

  • provide, operate, secure, and improve our website, enterprise portal, APIs, integrations, reports, and support;
  • connect customer-authorized agricultural systems and process operational data;
  • generate irrigation intelligence, water-use insights, field operations visibility, compliance-support workflows, and enterprise reports;
  • understand what was recommended, scheduled, applied, verified, or reported across agricultural workflows;
  • communicate with users, customers, prospects, partners, and support contacts;
  • respond to onboarding forms, demo requests, technical questions, and customer support needs;
  • monitor system security, prevent misuse, and debug technical issues;
  • improve AGRO-AI products, workflows, models, and infrastructure;
  • comply with legal, contractual, security, and operational obligations.

3. Customer-authorized integrations

AGRO-AI is designed to work with systems customers already use. When you connect a third-party platform, you authorize AGRO-AI to access and process the data allowed by that connection.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights and permissions needed to connect third-party systems, submit data, and authorize AGRO-AI to process that data.

AGRO-AI does not control third-party platforms. Their own terms, permissions, APIs, data practices, and availability may apply.

4. AI, analytics, and model use

AGRO-AI uses data to generate agricultural intelligence, recommendations, analysis, reporting, and workflow support.

AGRO-AI does not sell customer operational data.

AGRO-AI does not use identifiable customer operational data to train unrelated third-party foundation models unless authorized by the customer or required to provide the requested service under appropriate safeguards.

We may use aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized data to improve product performance, benchmarks, analytics, infrastructure reliability, and service quality, provided such data does not identify a specific customer, user, or operation.

5. How we share information

AGRO-AI may share information with:

Service providers

We may use vendors for hosting, analytics, security, infrastructure, communications, support, billing, and other operational needs.

Customer-authorized integrations

When a customer connects a third-party platform or requests an integration workflow, information may be exchanged with that system as needed to provide the service.

Enterprise customers and authorized users

Information may be visible to authorized users within the same organization, account, project, farm, district, or enterprise workspace.

Legal and safety purposes

We may disclose information if required to comply with law, enforce agreements, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or respond to lawful requests.

Business transfers

If AGRO-AI is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

6. Cookies and analytics

AGRO-AI may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand usage, improve performance, and protect security. Users may adjust browser settings to limit cookies, although some features may not work properly without them.

7. Data retention

AGRO-AI retains information for as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal or contractual obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, support audits, and operate the business.

Customer operational data may be retained according to the applicable customer agreement, enterprise workspace settings, integration configuration, or legal requirements.

8. Security

AGRO-AI uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers and users are responsible for maintaining secure credentials, managing access permissions, and promptly notifying AGRO-AI of suspected unauthorized use.

9. International data processing

AGRO-AI may process information in the United States and other locations where we or our service providers operate. By using AGRO-AI services, you understand that information may be processed outside your country or region, subject to applicable safeguards.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have rights to request portability or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact AGRO-AI using the contact information below. We may need to verify your identity and authority before fulfilling a request.

11. Children's privacy

AGRO-AI services are intended for business, agricultural, enterprise, and professional use. They are not directed to children.

12. Third-party links and platforms

AGRO-AI websites and services may link to third-party websites, platforms, APIs, or services. AGRO-AI is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party services.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
