Third-party data handling

Subprocessors are not the same as customer-authorized integrations.

AGRO-AI distinguishes vendors that help us operate the service from external agricultural or enterprise systems that a customer chooses to connect. Both require governance, but the authority, data flow and contractual relationship can be different.

Public register · Version 2026-09 · Last updated September 14, 2026

1. How we define the categories

  • Subprocessor / service provider: a third party AGRO-AI engages to process Customer Data or personal information on our behalf in order to operate a service.
  • Infrastructure provider: a provider of hosting, edge, storage, network, security or similar infrastructure. Depending on the service and legal role, an infrastructure provider may also be a subprocessor.
  • Model provider: an external model or transcription provider that may receive customer-authorized context for inference where configured.
  • Customer-authorized integration: a third-party system the customer directs AGRO-AI to connect to, such as an equipment, agronomic, satellite, storage, accounting or enterprise platform. The customer may have its own direct agreement with that provider.

Scope matters. A company name appearing below does not mean every AGRO-AI customer or every product sends data to that provider. Actual provider use depends on the service, enabled features, selected integrations and customer configuration.

3. Customer-authorized integrations

AGRO-AI can connect to external agricultural, equipment, satellite, storage, collaboration, accounting or enterprise systems when the customer authorizes the integration. Examples can include equipment operations platforms, irrigation/controller systems, file-storage providers, productivity suites and other agricultural data sources.

These integrations are governed differently from ordinary subprocessors:

  • The customer initiates or authorizes the connection.
  • Requested OAuth/API scopes should follow least privilege.
  • AGRO-AI should document whether the connector is read-only, can write data back or can initiate actions.
  • Credentials and refresh tokens are treated as secrets, not ordinary model context.
  • Disconnecting an integration should revoke future access; retained copies already imported into AGRO-AI remain subject to the applicable retention/deletion rules.
  • The third-party provider's own privacy, security and account terms may also apply to the customer's relationship with that provider.

4. Vendor approval requirements

Before a provider receives Customer Data, personal information, Restricted Operational Data or credentials in production, AGRO-AI's governance standard calls for review appropriate to risk. That review should cover:

  • purpose and data categories;
  • security and confidentiality controls;
  • incident and breach obligations;
  • retention and deletion behavior;
  • AI/model training or secondary-use terms where relevant;
  • processing locations and international-transfer implications;
  • downstream subprocessors;
  • contract/DPA status;
  • customer notice or authorization requirements; and
  • the internal owner and next review date.

5. Model providers receive additional review

Because model APIs can introduce unique questions about training, retention and context scope, a model provider is not approved merely because it has a secure API. AGRO-AI also evaluates submitted-data use, model retention, available no-storage/zero-retention controls, endpoint-specific settings and whether the provider's terms permit the intended enterprise use.

AGRO-AI will not state that a model provider has “zero retention” unless the exact account, endpoint and configuration qualify.

6. Changes to subprocessors

AGRO-AI may add, replace or remove providers as the service evolves. Where a customer agreement or applicable law requires notice, objection rights or an updated DPA schedule, the applicable process will govern. This page is dated so customers can identify the public version they reviewed.

7. Government and legal requests

AGRO-AI may disclose data when required by applicable law or valid legal process. Where legally permitted and appropriate, we seek to limit disclosures to what is required and to preserve customer confidentiality. We do not treat informal requests for customer operational data as equivalent to valid legal authority.

8. Request the current enterprise provider schedule

For a deployment-specific subprocessor list, DPA schedule or provider data-flow question, contact contact@agroai-pilot.com and identify the relevant AGRO-AI product and organization.
