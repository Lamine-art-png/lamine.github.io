Privacy Notice

Privacy at AGRO-AI.

This notice explains how AGRO-AI Inc. handles personal information across our public website, Enterprise Portal, Platform API, Field Intelligence workflows, support, business communications and customer-authorized integrations.

1. Scope

This Privacy Notice applies when AGRO-AI determines the purposes and means of processing personal information in connection with agroai-pilot.com, account registration, business communications, demonstrations, support, the AGRO-AI Enterprise Portal, the AGRO-AI Platform API and related first-party services.

When AGRO-AI processes personal information on behalf of an enterprise customer, the customer may be the controller, business or equivalent decision-maker and AGRO-AI may act as a processor or service provider. In that situation, the customer's privacy notice and the applicable commercial agreement or Data Processing Addendum may control the processing.

Agricultural operational data is broader than personal information. Field boundaries, machine telemetry, irrigation data, yield records and other farm information may be commercially sensitive even when privacy law does not classify it as personal information. See our Data Governance Standard for how we govern those data classes.

2. Our role

AGRO-AI may act in different legal roles depending on the processing activity. We generally act as an independent business/controller for our own account administration, website operations, security, business communications and support. We may act as a processor/service provider when an enterprise customer directs us to process personal information within its organization, connected systems or workflows.

We do not use a role label to expand our rights over Customer Data. Rights and obligations are defined by the applicable product terms, enterprise agreement and DPA where relevant.

4. Where information comes from

  • Directly from you when you register, contact us, upload content, use Field Intelligence or configure a workflow.
  • From your organization and authorized administrators.
  • From customer-authorized connectors and third-party systems.
  • Automatically from use of our websites, applications, API, security controls and infrastructure.
  • From service providers that support identity, security, infrastructure, billing, communications or support.
  • From public or business sources where permitted by law and appropriate for business-to-business operations.

5. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, maintain and support AGRO-AI products and contracted services.
  • Authenticate users, verify organizations and enforce permissions.
  • Connect customer-authorized systems and process customer-directed workflows.
  • Generate recommendations, reports, summaries, evidence views, alerts and other requested intelligence.
  • Protect accounts, credentials, data and infrastructure; investigate misuse and security incidents.
  • Measure service usage, reliability and performance.
  • Provide billing, account administration and customer support.
  • Communicate about operational, security, legal, support and product matters.
  • Improve service quality using information we are authorized to use for that purpose.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements and protect legal rights.

We do not use customer operational farm data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Legal bases where applicable

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR require a legal basis, AGRO-AI may rely on performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent or compliance with legal obligations depending on the activity. Legitimate interests may include securing and operating the service, preventing fraud, improving reliability, supporting customers and managing business relationships. Where consent is the required basis, it can be withdrawn subject to applicable law.

7. When information may be disclosed

AGRO-AI may disclose information only for legitimate, authorized purposes, including:

  • Service providers and subprocessors that provide infrastructure, hosting, storage, security, model inference, communications, support, billing or other service functions under appropriate obligations.
  • Customer-authorized integrations when the customer directs AGRO-AI to exchange data with a connected system.
  • Your organization and its authorized administrators and users.
  • Professional advisers such as legal, accounting or security advisers where necessary and subject to appropriate duties.
  • Legal and safety disclosures where required by applicable law, valid legal process or necessary to protect rights, security or safety.
  • Corporate transactions such as a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization or sale, subject to applicable confidentiality and legal requirements.

AGRO-AI's product policy is not to sell Customer Data. We do not authorize a service provider to use Customer Data for its own independent advertising purposes merely because it processes the data for AGRO-AI.

See Subprocessors & Integrations for our third-party governance approach.

8. AI, models and automated processing

Some AGRO-AI features use machine learning or generative model providers to analyze customer-authorized context and produce outputs. The data sent to a model depends on the product and task.

  • AGRO-AI's policy is that Customer Data is not used to train shared AGRO-AI models or third-party foundation models for unrelated customers unless the customer expressly authorizes that use through an appropriate agreement or control.
  • Connector credentials and secrets must not be placed in model context.
  • Tenant isolation and authorization are applied before retrieval and context assembly.
  • Uploaded files, notes, transcripts and evidence are treated as untrusted data, not instructions that can override system policy.
  • Where an output could affect a consequential operational decision, AGRO-AI designs for authorization, review and traceability rather than treating model output as automatic authority.

More detail is available in the AI Data Use Policy.

9. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, including service delivery, security, auditability, dispute resolution, legal compliance and legitimate operational needs. Retention can differ by data class, product and customer agreement.

Deletion may occur on different schedules across active systems, derived indexes, logs, backups and legally required records. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup unless that behavior is supported by the relevant infrastructure and agreement. Backup copies may age out under controlled retention schedules and remain inaccessible for ordinary product use while retained.

Enterprise customers can address product-specific return/deletion obligations in the applicable agreement or DPA.

10. Security

AGRO-AI uses technical and organizational measures designed to protect information appropriate to the nature of the service and risk. Our security program addresses access control, credential management, tenant isolation, server-side secrets, transport security, logging, monitoring, vulnerability management, backups/recovery, incident response and secure change practices.

No security program can guarantee absolute security. Users and organizations are responsible for protecting their credentials and promptly reporting suspected compromise.

We do not claim a certification or audit result unless it has actually been issued and is in scope. See our Security Program.

11. International data transfers

AGRO-AI is based in the United States and may use service providers or infrastructure in multiple countries. As a result, personal information may be processed outside the country where it was collected.

Where applicable law requires a transfer mechanism, AGRO-AI will use an appropriate mechanism through the applicable agreement or DPA. Depending on the jurisdiction and arrangement, this may include contractual safeguards or another lawful mechanism. Customer-specific transfer details can depend on the selected service, integration and provider configuration.

12. Privacy rights and requests

Depending on your location, the applicable law and AGRO-AI's role, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, information about processing, withdrawal of consent, or an appeal of certain decisions. Some U.S. state laws may also provide rights related to sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling.

To submit a request, email contact@agroai-pilot.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” Please identify the relevant AGRO-AI account or organization and the nature of the request. We may need to verify identity and authority before acting.

If AGRO-AI processes the relevant data solely on behalf of an enterprise customer, we may direct the request to that customer and assist it as required by law and contract.

Authorized agents may submit requests where applicable law permits. We may ask for proof of authorization and may still need to verify the individual involved.

13. California and other U.S. state privacy disclosures

Residents of certain U.S. states may have privacy rights that vary by jurisdiction. Depending on applicability, these rights can include access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out rights and appeal rights.

AGRO-AI does not use customer operational farm data for cross-context behavioral advertising. If website technologies ever create a legally defined “sale” or “sharing” obligation under an applicable state law, AGRO-AI will provide the legally required notice and choice mechanism rather than treating product operational data as advertising inventory.

We do not discriminate against a person for exercising applicable privacy rights.

14. Brazil (LGPD)

Where Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados applies, data subjects may have rights provided by the LGPD, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization/blocking/deletion where applicable, portability, information about sharing, consent information and review/objection rights in circumstances established by law.

International transfers involving personal data are handled according to the applicable legal mechanism and the relevant customer/provider arrangement. Enterprise customers may request additional information through the DPA process.

15. EEA, Switzerland and United Kingdom

Where European data-protection law applies, individuals may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. Where AGRO-AI relies on consent, the individual may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing.

Where required, international transfer safeguards are addressed through the applicable legal mechanism and agreement. AGRO-AI will not claim a specific transfer framework applies to a customer unless it is actually implemented for that processing.

16. Children

AGRO-AI's enterprise and developer services are intended for business and professional users and are not directed to children. Do not submit a child's personal information unless the processing has been specifically authorized and lawfully arranged for the relevant service.

17. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Notice as our services, providers, practices or legal obligations change. We will update the version and date on this page. Where a material change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law or contract, we will use an appropriate process.

18. Contact

Privacy, data-protection and enterprise DPA questions can be sent to contact@agroai-pilot.com. Include the organization, product and request type so the request can be routed appropriately.
