AGRO-AI Academy is open. The future is yours to learn.

Our first full agricultural AI course is live, free to study, and built around something more important than hype: helping people understand the intelligence changing their industry.

Thursday, October 29, 2026 · 8:00 a.m. PDT·San Francisco, California·AGRO-AI
Start learning free ↗Explore courses

A grower can spend an entire morning making decisions about crops, water, weather, equipment and labor without ever using the phrase “artificial intelligence.” Those decisions are precisely why understanding AI matters.

Models are arriving in irrigation systems, satellite services, market tools and farm-management software. More information is available than ever. Yet access to more information does not automatically make a decision easier. Someone still has to judge the quality of the data, understand what a forecast can tell them, recognize when a recommendation is weak, and decide what to do.

That is where education belongs.

Today, AGRO-AI Academy opens its first complete course: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture. Anyone can enroll. There is no tuition, no subscription requirement and no obligation to become an AGRO-AI software customer. Learners progress at their own pace and can earn a digital certificate after completing the course and passing the assessment.

The people building the future of agriculture should never be the only people who understand its technology.

From an idea to an actual classroom

Earlier this month, we introduced the thinking behind AGRO-AI Academy: people working in agriculture deserve education that is practical, accessible and grounded in the realities of the field. We had heard the need repeatedly. A new tool could be powerful and still leave the person responsible for using it asking basic, sensible questions.

Today's announcement is different. The classroom is open. The lessons are available. Assessments work. Learners can finish the course and earn an independently verifiable credential.

We built the first learning experience around the questions someone should be comfortable asking before trusting an AI output. What data did the system use? Is it current? What assumptions produced the prediction? How confident is the recommendation? Did the action happen, and did it have the intended effect?

These are not academic questions reserved for engineers. They are the beginning of responsible operational use.

Across four modules, the course follows AI from basic definitions to data and models, then into real agricultural decisions and the questions that make technology trustworthy.

MODULE 01

What AI means for agriculture

Understand AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative systems. Learn the difference between rules written in advance and systems that learn patterns from data, and why the distinction matters in the field.

MODULE 02

How agricultural AI works

See how field observations, satellite imagery, sensor readings and operational records become model inputs. Explore prediction, classification, forecasting and optimization—and how recommendations need evidence and human judgment.

MODULE 03

AI across the season

Follow examples from crop planning and irrigation to remote sensing, crop condition, harvest and market decisions. Learn what vegetation indices and water estimates can reveal, and why they cannot tell the whole story on their own.

MODULE 04

Using AI responsibly

Learn how to challenge recommendations, recognize uncertainty and poor-quality data, protect farm information, and keep responsibility with people. Finish with a practical exercise and a course summary.

Lessons are short, structured and self-paced. Learners can save progress, revisit explanations, keep notes, and use the practice quizzes to check their understanding before taking the final assessment.

A certificate that can actually be verified

At the end of the required lessons, learners can take the course's final examination. It draws 20 questions from a larger question bank, allows 30 minutes, and requires a score of at least 80% to pass. Practice quizzes support learning but do not determine certification. If a learner does not pass, they can review the material and retry after the stated waiting period.

A successful learner receives an AGRO-AI Academy certificate with a unique credential ID. The certificate can be downloaded as a PDF, added to a professional profile, and checked through a public verification page. The credential records the completion of real coursework and an assessment; it is not an academic degree or an accredited professional license.

That distinction matters. We want learners to know exactly what they have achieved, and others to be able to verify it.

Future program courses

How will new courses be released?

We intend to build a regular, expanding catalog. But a curriculum is only valuable if the material deserves the learner’s time. We will not promise a new course every fourteen days before that pace has been proven sustainable.

New courses will be published when their instruction, assessment design, technical delivery and necessary review are ready. We will share updates as courses open and keep the public catalog clear about what is available versus planned.

Among the areas we are preparing to expand next are deeper Academy foundations and practical learning for AGRO-AI Enterprise Portal users. Platform API learning will follow as the developer curriculum matures. Course order and dates will be announced when confirmed.

Accessible across borders, with honest language labels

Agriculture spans languages, regions and very different kinds of operations. A learner should not need to live near a technology hub to start understanding agricultural AI.

The Academy offers a 60-language interface, and we are extending translated learning materials. Where a lesson has been machine-translated, it is identified as such; machine-generated content is not represented as human-reviewed instruction. Certification assessments remain in English unless a translation has been reviewed and approved for examination use.

That is the approach we will continue taking: expand access while being clear about the quality and status of each piece of content.

An Academy open to everyone

A farmer curious about a new irrigation tool. An agronomist who wants to challenge a crop-health prediction. A student entering agriculture. A field operator expected to use unfamiliar software. A developer trying to understand agricultural workflows. A manager responsible for decisions that technology can support but never own.

All of them belong here.

AGRO-AI Academy is connected to the wider AGRO-AI ecosystem, but participation does not depend on an enterprise contract. No one has to purchase the Enterprise Portal or Platform API to enroll in the Academy.

Our hope is that people leave with a vocabulary they can use, questions they know how to ask, and stronger judgment when technology reaches their work.

The first course is here. The institution we want to build around it is only beginning.

Today, on October 29, 2026, we are proud to open this experience to the world.
