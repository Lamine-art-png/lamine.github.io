Product update · Field Intelligence

Field Intelligence now connects the field visit to the operating decision.

AGRO-AI has expanded Field Intelligence with live visual analysis, multilingual transcription, stronger evidence handling, linked tasks, verification and decision memory so a field observation can move through the operation without losing its source.

AGRO-AI•San Francisco, California•Wednesday, August 26, 2026•6 minute read

AGRO-AI Field Intelligence is moving from field capture to a governed field-to-action operating loop. A team can capture what is happening, preserve where and when it happened, analyze live field context, carry the evidence into AGRO-AI intelligence, create accountable work and keep the result connected to verification.

What changed
  • Near-real-time visual analysis can support a live field inspection while the durable recording continues.
  • Live multilingual transcription adds a second intelligence stream to voice-first field capture.
  • Observations can carry field, block, crop, location, transcript, media, confidence and evidence context into Ask AGRO-AI.
  • A field observation can become a linked task and stay connected through execution and verification.
  • Field State and Decision Memory preserve what was known, what changed and why a decision changed.

See and transcribe more while the field visit is happening.

Field Intelligence can now analyze sampled camera frames during a recording instead of waiting for the full upload to finish. That gives field teams near-real-time visual support while they are still looking at the crop, equipment or physical condition in front of them.

Live transcription runs alongside the capture workflow. Browser speech recognition can provide the lowest-latency caption path when it is available, while a server-side multilingual lane can support transcription and correction. The result is a field record that can carry the spoken explanation together with the visual and location context.

Live outputs are deliberately marked as preliminary. The durable uploaded recording remains the authoritative source for the final evidence trail, and consequential conclusions still require human review.

Live visual context

Sample camera frames during the visit and return preliminary visual findings without interrupting the recording.

Multilingual speech

Capture live transcript context so the operator's explanation does not disappear after the field walk.

Location and crop context

Keep field, block, crop, coordinates, event type and timing attached to the observation.

Durable evidence

Preserve the uploaded capture as the source record used after the live inspection ends.

Separate what was observed from what the system thinks it means.

The intelligence layer behind Field Intelligence now treats evidence more carefully. Information can be separated into observed facts, derived information, hypotheses, unknowns and conflicts instead of presenting every model output as if it were equally certain.

Evidence is checked for scope and provenance before reasoning. Duplicate evidence and records from the wrong tenant, workspace or field are excluded. Unsupported operational numbers can be rejected before they become recommendations.

This is especially important in decisions such as irrigation, where missing calibration, flow, pressure, area, timing or other required inputs should stop the system from inventing precision. AGRO-AI is designed to fail closed when the evidence is not strong enough.

The operating loop
Observe → preserve evidence → understand → recommend → approve → assign → execute → verify → remember

An observation can now become accountable work.

One of the central changes is the link between Field Intelligence and Tasks. A field observation can create a task while preserving its source observation ID. The task can point back to the field record, and the field record can stay connected to the work created from it.

That closes a common operational gap. Seeing a problem is useful. Knowing who owns the response, what evidence supported the decision and whether the work was completed is much more useful.

Ask AGRO-AI can reason over the field observation without making the user repeat it.

When a Field Intelligence observation is active, its evidence can move into the AGRO-AI reasoning context. That can include field and block context, crop, event type, severity, transcript, visible facts, hypotheses, uncertainties, recommended follow-up, media moments, confidence and source evidence identifiers.

The goal is continuity. A manager should be able to open an observation and ask what it means, what is missing, what should happen next and why, without manually rebuilding the field story in a separate chat.

Remember what changed, not only the latest answer.

Field Intelligence is also connected to the new Field State and Decision Memory architecture inside the Enterprise Portal. The system can preserve immutable state revisions, decision snapshots, approval and execution events, verification evidence and a history of why the operating recommendation changed.

This gives teams a stronger answer to practical questions: What did we know at the time? What new evidence arrived? Why did the recommendation change? What did the team approve? Was the action completed? What evidence shows the result?

Capture the field truth once. Use it across the operation.

The upgraded workflow is designed so one observation can support intelligence, tasks, verification, reporting and later review instead of being copied into several disconnected systems.

Human control remains part of the product.

AGRO-AI can help surface likely issues, confidence, uncertainty and recommended next steps, but live field guidance is not treated as a substitute for agronomic judgment. The system preserves review gates around consequential actions and keeps source evidence visible so teams can challenge a recommendation when the facts do not support it.

That is the direction of Field Intelligence: faster field capture, stronger context and a tighter connection between what people see, what AGRO-AI understands and what the operation actually does next.

Open Field Intelligence in the Enterprise Portal.

Field Intelligence is part of the AGRO-AI Enterprise Portal and is designed for operators, advisors and enterprise teams that need field observations to become usable evidence and accountable work.
