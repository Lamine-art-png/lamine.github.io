AGRO-AI Marks Major Research Milestone with UC Merced and the University of Arizona

A $1 million proposed multi-state research effort brings together agricultural AI, robotics expertise, and specialty-crop science.

San Francisco, California·Friday, October 16, 2026 · 8:00 a.m. PDT·AGRO-AI

AGRO-AI has reached a significant research milestone alongside the University of California, Merced and the University of Arizona, with the formal submission of a $1 million multi-state proposal under the USDA Specialty Crop Multi-State Program.

Focused on AI-enabled cantaloupe harvest maturity and sizing, the collaboration brings together agricultural robotics expertise, crop science and applied artificial intelligence to advance harvest assessment and practical decision-making for American specialty-crop growers.

For AGRO-AI, this collaboration reflects a growing role within the agricultural research and innovation ecosystem, strengthening the connection between scientific expertise and technology built for real-world farming operations.

AGRO-AI extends its appreciation to Professor Reza Ehsani and the participating teams at UC Merced and the University of Arizona for their leadership and contributions to this shared milestone.
