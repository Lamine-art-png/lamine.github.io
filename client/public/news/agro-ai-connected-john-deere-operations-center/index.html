AGRO-AI connects with John Deere Operations Center™

Customer-authorized field, boundary, machine, and work data can move into AGRO-AI agricultural intelligence workflows.

AGRO-AI
San Francisco, California · Tuesday, July 28, 2026 at 8:00 AM PDT

AGRO-AI is now connected with John Deere Operations Center™. The connection creates a customer-authorized path from operational data into AGRO-AI workflows for intelligence, reporting, recommendations, and verification.

Agricultural operations do not suffer from a shortage of data. They suffer from broken continuity between the systems that collect it and the workflows where teams have to act on it. Field boundaries may live in one system. Machine and work records may live in another. Reports, compliance evidence, recommendations, and follow-up actions are often assembled somewhere else, frequently by hand.

The connection is designed to close part of that gap. With customer authorization, AGRO-AI can use field, boundary, machine, and work data from Operations Center inside its agricultural intelligence workflows. The result is not a replacement for the systems already in use. It is a more direct path from existing operational context to the decisions, records, and actions that follow.

Why the connection matters

A system becomes more valuable when its data can carry forward. For teams that use Operations Center, the connection can reduce the need to reconstruct field and machine context inside a separate platform. That continuity can strengthen recommendations, simplify recurring reporting, support field verification, and create a clearer record of what was planned, observed, and completed.

A connection alone is not the product. The value appears when authorized data is used inside a workflow.

Built around authorization, control, and existing infrastructure

Access is initiated through the user's Operations Center connection. AGRO-AI is now visible within Connections in John Deere Operations Center™. In addition, eligible AGRO-AI users can initiate the connection from the AGRO-AI integrations experience.

AGRO-AI is built to complement existing agricultural systems rather than force a rip-and-replace decision. Its role is to connect across operational sources, understand context, recommend what comes next, and preserve evidence of what happened. The Operations Center connection extends that architecture to data already used by agricultural teams in the field.

From data access to operational intelligence

In AGRO-AI, authorized data can add field and machine context to recommendations, support customer-ready reports with less manual assembly, strengthen compliance-readiness workflows, and make field activity easier to verify across teams.

Water was AGRO-AI's entry point, but the underlying problem is broader: agriculture is a coordination and decision problem. The same operation may depend on machines, controllers, weather, files, people, and field observations. Intelligence becomes useful when it can work across those boundaries.

Availability

AGRO-AI is now visible within Connections in John Deere Operations Center™. Eligible AGRO-AI users can initiate the connection from the AGRO-AI integrations experience. Access remains subject to user authorization, account eligibility, and the scopes enabled for the AGRO-AI application.
