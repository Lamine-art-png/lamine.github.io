AGRO-AI is now connected with John Deere Operations Center™.

Eligible AGRO-AI users can bring authorized Operations Center context into one operating environment for recommendations, reporting, verification, and Field Intelligence.

AGRO-AI is now connected with John Deere Operations Center™. The connection gives eligible users a direct path to bring authorized operational context into AGRO-AI without rebuilding their workflow around another disconnected system.

Growers and agricultural enterprises already manage important information across equipment platforms, controller systems, weather tools, field records, spreadsheets, and conversations. The hard part is rarely collecting one more signal. The hard part is turning the context that already exists into a clear recommendation, an accountable next step, and a record of what happened.

The Operations Center connection advances that operating model. It allows approved context to support AGRO-AI workflows for recommendations, reporting, verification, and Field Intelligence, based on the user’s authorization, permissions, and available data.

The value of a connection is what becomes possible after the data arrives.

Built around the decisions growers already have to make.

A connection should reduce friction between information and action. Inside the AGRO-AI Enterprise Portal, connected context can be organized alongside other authorized agricultural sources and field evidence, helping teams build a more coherent view of the operation.

How access works

Access is initiated through the user's Operations Center connection. AGRO-AI is now visible within Connections in John Deere Operations Center™. In addition, eligible AGRO-AI users can initiate the connection from the AGRO-AI integrations experience.

1

Open the connection

Begin from Connections in Operations Center or from the Integrations experience inside AGRO-AI.

2

Authorize access

Review the requested permissions and approve the connection through the user’s account.

3

Use the context

Bring eligible connected information into AGRO-AI workflows for intelligence, evidence, reporting, and verification.

Field evidence becomes more useful when it has operational context.

Field Intelligence allows operators to capture observations through voice, text, images, and location. When field evidence is reviewed alongside authorized connected context, teams can preserve more of the reasoning behind a decision and keep the evidence attached to the resulting work.

The aim is practical: shorten the distance between what the operation knows and what the team can act on. AGRO-AI keeps recommendations, approvals, assignments, reports, and verification inside the same governed environment.

Designed to work above existing agricultural systems.

AGRO-AI is built as an intelligence layer above the infrastructure agricultural teams already use. The Operations Center connection follows that principle. Growers can continue working with their existing systems while using AGRO-AI to organize context, generate decision support, preserve evidence, and improve operating visibility.

Connection availability and the information accessible through it depend on the user’s account eligibility, authorization, permissions, and applicable terms.
