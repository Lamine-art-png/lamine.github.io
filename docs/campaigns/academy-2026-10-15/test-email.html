Hello,

We’re excited to introduce AGRO-AI Academy, our new educational initiative for farmers, field operators, agricultural managers, developers, and anyone looking to understand how AI can support their work.

The Academy grew from what we saw while bringing AGRO-AI into real agricultural operations: the people choosing new technology and the teams using it every day often need different kinds of training. We want to make that learning easier to access.

We’ll begin with practical video lessons for the AGRO-AI Enterprise Portal, then expand into the Platform API and broader applications of artificial intelligence across agriculture.

Our first lessons are planned for October 29, 2026. The educational resources will be free.
