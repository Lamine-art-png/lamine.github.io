# AGRO-AI Privacy Notice v2

**Status:** DRAFT — LEGAL AND OPERATIONS REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION  
**Proposed effective date:** To be set only after approval  
**Entity:** AGRO-AI Inc.

This Privacy Notice explains how AGRO-AI Inc. ("AGRO-AI," "we," "us") handles personal data in connection with our websites, enterprise applications, developer services, support, sales, business operations, and other services that link to this Notice.

This Notice is about **personal data**. Customer farm, field, equipment, agronomic, operational, and other business data may also be governed by the applicable customer agreement, Data Processing Addendum, and AGRO-AI Farm Data Trust Covenant. Some operational data may also be personal data depending on its content and applicable law.

## 1. Data we may process

Depending on the service and relationship, we may process:

- **Account and identity data:** name, work email, organization, role, authentication and account-verification information.
- **Business relationship data:** employer or organization, job title, sales and partnership communications, meeting and support history.
- **Billing and transaction data:** subscription, invoice, payment status, and limited payment-related metadata. Payment card data may be handled directly by payment providers rather than stored by AGRO-AI.
- **Technical and security data:** IP address, user agent, device/browser information, timestamps, request identifiers, authentication events, audit events, security signals, and service logs.
- **Usage data:** product features used, API operations, job status, service performance, usage units, and similar operational telemetry.
- **Support and communications data:** content submitted through support, contact forms, email, or other authorized communications.
- **Field and operational data that includes personal data:** for example, geolocation tied to an identifiable operator, voice recordings, photographs containing identifiable people, or records associated with named personnel.
- **Integration data:** information received from a third-party service when an authorized user connects that service to AGRO-AI.
- **Recruiting data:** information submitted by applicants, including resume/CV, contact details, work samples, education, and professional history.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** where used, information described in the applicable cookie notice or consent interface.

**Verification required:** Complete the production data inventory before publication. Remove categories not collected and add material categories that are collected.

## 2. Where data comes from

We may receive personal data directly from you; from your organization; through your use of AGRO-AI services; from customer-authorized integrations; from infrastructure, security, communications, payment, analytics, and support providers; from public professional sources; and from other sources permitted by law.

## 3. Why we process personal data

Depending on context and applicable law, we may process personal data to:

- provide, operate, authenticate, secure, and support the services;
- create and administer accounts, organizations, projects, integrations, and credentials;
- process subscriptions, billing, and commercial relationships;
- respond to inquiries, support requests, demos, partnerships, and customer communications;
- detect abuse, investigate incidents, prevent fraud, and protect systems and users;
- maintain reliability, auditability, provenance, and service performance;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce legal rights;
- improve services using information we are permitted to use for that purpose;
- recruit and evaluate candidates;
- send product, operational, security, or marketing communications as permitted by law and user preferences.

Where law requires a legal basis, the applicable basis may include performance of a contract, steps requested before entering a contract, legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations, protection of vital interests, or consent. The exact basis depends on the processing activity and jurisdiction.

**Verification required:** Build and approve a processing register mapping each material purpose to categories, systems, retention, recipients, and legal basis where applicable.

## 4. Customer-controlled processing

When an enterprise customer uses AGRO-AI to process personal data under the customer's instructions, the customer may act as the controller/business and AGRO-AI may act as processor/service provider, depending on applicable law and the specific processing.

In those cases, the customer agreement and Data Processing Addendum may govern the processing. Individuals may need to direct rights requests to the relevant customer, and AGRO-AI will provide required assistance under applicable law and contract.

AGRO-AI may act independently as a controller/business for limited activities such as its own account administration, security, billing, legal compliance, website operations, and direct business relationships where applicable.

## 5. Customer operational data and AI

AGRO-AI distinguishes personal-data privacy from broader agricultural data governance. Farm and operational data that is not personal data can still be commercially sensitive.

The AGRO-AI Farm Data Trust Covenant and AI & Model Data Use Policy are intended to explain how AGRO-AI handles Customer Operational Data, model inference, training, aggregation, portability, and related controls.

**Do not publish a categorical model-training promise here until the AI data-flow and provider settings are verified.**

We may disclose personal data to service providers and subprocessors that support hosting, storage, security, communications, analytics, payments, support, professional services, and customer-authorized integrations; to affiliated entities where applicable; to professional advisers; to authorities or other parties where required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety; and in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets subject to applicable protections.

A production Subprocessor Registry should identify material vendors that process covered customer personal data.

## 7. International transfers

AGRO-AI and its service providers may process data in countries other than the country where the data originated. Where applicable law requires a transfer mechanism, AGRO-AI will use an appropriate mechanism and contractual safeguards.

For covered EU/EEA or UK transfers, the applicable DPA should identify the relevant transfer mechanism. For covered Brazilian personal-data transfers, the DPA and operational program should account for applicable LGPD and ANPD international-transfer requirements.

**Verification required:** Map actual processing locations, subprocessors, and transfer mechanisms before publication.

## 8. Retention

We retain personal data only for periods justified by the purpose, customer agreement, security needs, dispute resolution, financial/accounting requirements, legal obligations, and other legitimate operational needs.

Retention may differ by category. Deletion from active systems may not mean immediate deletion from backups where backups are maintained on a defined rotation and protected from ordinary use.

**Verification required:** Publish or link to actual enforceable retention rules. Do not invent fixed periods that production systems do not implement.

## 9. Security

AGRO-AI uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. The measures appropriate to a service may include access controls, tenant boundaries, credential management, logging, encryption, vulnerability management, backup/recovery, incident response, change management, and confidentiality obligations.

No service can guarantee absolute security. Security claims on the Trust Center must match implemented controls and evidence. Certifications and audit reports must not be claimed before they are actually issued and applicable.

Rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. AGRO-AI will need to verify requests and may need information sufficient to identify the relevant records.
